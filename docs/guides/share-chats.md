# Share a chat with your workspace

In a team workspace, you can share a chat you own with every workspace member.
This includes people who join later and messages saved after you share it.

To share a chat:

1. In the chat header, select **Share**.
2. Review the audience notice, then select **Share with workspace**.
3. Select **Copy link** to copy the chat address.

Everyone in this workspace can read this chat, including future messages.
Results may include information from your personal connections. Sharing does
not give others access to those connections.

## Share every conversation by default

Workspace owners and admins can share new conversations with the team from
the start:

1. In **Workspace Settings > Details**, turn on **Conversations shared by default**.
2. Select **Save Changes**.

New direct and scheduled conversations are shared with every member. Existing
conversations keep their audience. Turning the setting off also leaves
existing conversations unchanged. Conversation owners can still stop sharing
an individual chat. Personal workspaces do not offer this setting.

With default sharing on, the desktop and mobile sidebars include your own
conversations and shared conversations from other members, newest activity
first. Other people's conversations show their name and open for viewing.
They have no unread or approval badges. Saved conversations appear when the
sidebar next refreshes, including when you return to the app.

On New Conversation, before you send the first message, the composer shows
**Shared with everyone in WORKSPACE_NAME.** The notice uses your workspace's
name. Members and read-only members can check the setting in workspace details
but cannot change it.

## Read a shared chat

In the conversation list, select **Shared with workspace**. You can also open
a copied link and sign in. The link opens the matching workspace without
changing your default workspace. Only active members can read the chat.

The chat header shows a **View only** badge, who shared the chat, and when it
last refreshed. Only the chat owner can send messages. Select **Refresh** to
load saved messages added since your last refresh. The page also refreshes when you
return to it or reconnect. In a long chat, select **Load earlier messages** to
read older content. Refresh updates the messages you have loaded. Some tool details
or previews can be unavailable.
Files keep their separate workspace permissions.

## Stop sharing

In the chat's sharing controls, select **Stop sharing**. Workspace owners and
admins can also stop an existing share. Members lose access on their next
read. Copied text and downloaded files remain with their recipients.

Chats in personal workspaces and specialist child transcripts cannot be shared.
The specialist's saved result can appear in the shared parent chat.
