# Pricing and discount policy

Volume discount tiers are based on annual committed seats:

| Seats | Discount |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1–49 | 0% |
| 50–199 | 8% |
| 200–499 | 14% |
| 500+ | 20% |

Discounts above the listed tier require finance approval. Contract length does not automatically increase the volume discount.
